# Voice examples

**This is the highest-leverage file in the voice folder.** Agents imitate examples far better
than they follow adjectives. Paste 2–4 full pieces you wrote and like — blog posts, long
emails, internal docs, anything that sounds like you at your best. Prefer whole pieces over
fragments: openings and closings carry most of the voice.

For each example, add one line above it saying what to imitate ("this is how I open", "this is
my argument rhythm") and one line on anything NOT to imitate.
